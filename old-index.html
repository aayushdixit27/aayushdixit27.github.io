Senso receipt, staging origin

A verification receipt records two things about a piece of published content: proof it was allowed, meaning who approved it and under what authority, and proof it was right, meaning which claims were checked against which source documents and what could not be checked at all.

One receipt resolves here:

https://aayushdixit27.github.io/.well-known/senso-receipt/rcpt_cnt_8f21ab04_20260806T231000Z.json

Anyone holding the same source documents can recompute the checksums in that object and get the same score without trusting Senso. That is the point of it.

This is a staging origin and two things are not established.

The fixtures are synthetic. The booking, the dealer and the price sheet are invented for demonstration and describe no real customer or transaction.

The identity binding is not established. The issuer identity is senso.ai, and this copy is served from a personal GitHub Pages domain. UCP binds a namespace to whoever owns the domain, so ai.senso.verification only means something when served from senso.ai. Read this as a working demonstration of the format, not as a namespace claim.
